01 · What is Sody

An AI coach that adapts to the body you have today

Sody (Sound + Body) is an intelligent fitness coach for everyday people — including people with aches, chronic conditions, desk jobs, and rough days. Built on the principle that fitness should adapt to physical limits, Sody dynamically modifies every session so users build strength without risking injury. It generalizes beyond gym lifting to any physical activity, and it's social: friends back each other, share goals, and move together.

Position

Safety-first coaching

Not a logger with an AI sticker. The coach listens (pain, energy, sleep, cycle, flare-ups) before it prescribes.

Unit of intent

Goals

Everything orbits a goal: solo or shared, each keeps exactly one workout queued next. No "what now?".

Philosophy

Everything counts

A gym session, a run, a 3-minute desk stretch, a breathing break — all attributable to your goal.

02 · The core loop

One loop to design around

If the loop feels effortless, the app wins. Most of your design energy should go here.

Set a goal
→
Coach queues next workout
→
"How are you today?"
→
Plan adapts
→
Train & log fast
→
Recap · PRs · coach note
→
Share / attribute to goal
↻
Entry rules we protect: queued goal workouts start straight from Home with a lightweight "Adjust for today" (energy · pain · time). Everything else that generates a session goes through the daily check-in. Deliberate repeats (own routines, "Try this workout") skip both.

04 · Principles & your license to be critical

What we believe — challenge all of it

Principles

  • Today beats the plan. Sessions are built on today's check-in, not stale data.
  • One next thing. Home leads with a single hero action.
  • Everything counts. Any movement can serve a goal.
  • Progress, not ranking. Groups lift each other, never shame.
  • Health data is sacred. Never on public surfaces.
  • The coach shows its work. Reasons + research citations.
  • Logging faster than a notes app. One tap, one swipe.
  • Light + dark, every screen, day one.

Things we already suspect are wrong

  • Live session feels cramped — "too zoomed in", uncomfortable at first glance.
  • Check-in may be one step too long.
  • Home sections (next-up, desk, insights) compete for attention.
  • Pain body-map is coarse: whole muscle groups, not specific spots.
  • Empty states are copy-heavy; icons inconsistent across platforms.
  • The AI coach appears in 13 places with no unified identity.

You will find more. Kill our darlings — with a reason.

10 · Non-negotiable states

Every screen ships with all of these

Both themes

Light + dark variants, every screen. Theme = System/Light/Dark.

Loading

Skeleton-first on primary surfaces; the AI "thinking" state is a feature (10–30s waits must feel alive).

Empty

Copy-led with exactly one CTA. New users see these first — they are the app.

Error & recovery

Every AI failure has retry / fallback; never a dead end.

Accessibility

OS text-size compliance without clipping; reduce-motion variants of every celebration.

Privacy

Health data never public. Streak hidden everywhere public when weekly-activity is off.

11 · How we work

Working agreement

Where things live

  • Backlog: Trello list "user stories irene" on the Embody board — one card per epic. Comment, reorder, split.
  • Master doc: docs/design/irene/sody-user-stories.md (versioned; we sync Trello decisions back in).
  • This brief: docs/design/irene/sody-design-brief.html.
  • Old-design gallery: docs/design/irene/assets/research/.

Process

  • Design epic-by-epic (suggested order in the backlog). Flows over screens: happy path + states.
  • We review against acceptance criteria; you flag anything the backend must change — backend adapts to your design later, not the other way around.
  • Be brutal about the old UX. Every criticism with a proposed alternative is a gift.
  • Out of scope: backend/data model, marketing site, the BKOINZ Trello list (marketing ops).
