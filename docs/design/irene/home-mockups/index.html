HOME
MOCKUPS

Fifteen flowing, image-led takes on Home — no card stacks. Built from the Sody brand sheet: bubble display type, Lekton accents, lime + periwinkle + cream, photo overlays, and a living coach presence. Open any phone frame to explore.

01

Photo river

Full-bleed hero photo with the next workout floating on the image. Goals dissolve into the scroll.
02

Coach presence

Animated coach character greets Irene and points at today's session. Soft morphing blobs, no boxes.
03

Day ribbon

A continuous vertical ribbon of the day — morning, desk hours, evening — with photos stitching sections.
04

Liquid goals

Goals as breathing liquid orbs. Progress fills like water. Next workout sits inside the strongest orb.
05

Editorial

Magazine energy: oversized type, cropped photography, handwritten line overlays from the brand sheet.
06

Parallax stage

Immersive next-up stage with layered parallax photography and a sticky Start action.
07

Desk companion

For Dana at work: a soft character peeks in during desk hours with a 3-min reset woven into the flow.
08

Night pulse

Dark-mode home that still feels alive — neon lime accents, pulsing streaks, photo silhouettes.
09

Energy weather

Today's body state as weather. Atmosphere shifts with energy; the coach adapts the scene.
10

Story strip

Horizontal story moments for next-up, goals, desk, and week — swipeable, cinematic, image-first.
11

Body map flow

A living body illustration anchors the screen. Focus areas glow; next session maps onto the figure.
12

Celebration loop

Home after a completed session — confetti character, week pulse, and a soft invitation to start again.
13

Social pulse

Friends' movement rings above a cinematic next-up — progress without ranking.
14

Sound waves

Sound + Body made literal: waveforms, equalizer motion, loud SODY type.
15

Flare care

Soft home for flare-up / luteal days — caring character, gentler next session.
